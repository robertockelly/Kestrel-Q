# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to keep technical discussion respectful, specific and focused on the work.

Constructive disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats and deliberate disruption are not.

## Scope

This standard applies to project-controlled spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and community channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect constructive collaboration.

For a mature public release, the project may adopt a more detailed community code of conduct through a documented governance decision.
